What you can try: Each concept includes a homepage, live Shelterluv directory, adoption-process page, contact page, ways to help and a sample featured story. Resize your browser or open on your phone. Official-service links are identified; no preview application or payment form is provided.

Feedback that will help us choose.

  1. Which direction feels most like your rescue—and why?
  2. Can you find a dog, the adoption steps and the contact email easily?
  3. Which colors, typography and photo treatments would you keep?
  4. What information do adopters repeatedly ask you for?
  5. Which pages does your volunteer team update most often?

Send feedback to the person who shared this preview. There is no feedback form or tracking added here. Dog photos and profile facts are public-source snapshots, not promises of current availability. The live dog directory loads Shelterluv and its own services. These are browser prototypes, not an installed Squarespace theme.

Preview pages request no indexing. This is not password protection; anyone with a hosted link could view them.
